# Luma Drift 1.9 experience contract

Version 1.9 connects learning, useful help, earned rewards, optional encounters and companion expression. The main route's **1,000 L Journey, 240 M Remix and T event identities remain unchanged**. The release addresses specific path failures and adds optional variety; its synthetic audit does not claim a measured human retention improvement. See [the player audit](PLAYER_AUDIT_2026.md) for the evidence, assumptions and remaining L23 difficulty jump.

## Arrival and practice

A new guest can learn without creating a Google account. Existing local practice, progress and ownership remain local; a Google session is not a cross-device game-save promise. Seven short lessons use actual boards and require 27 real actions across exits, blockers, key/seal, manual gate, switch preview, Undo and hint. Continue cannot play the required puzzle action on the player's behalf.

Later/Skip remains available. The step and its board history are persisted together, so a skipped or interrupted practice can be resumed; completed practice can be replayed. Completion offers exactly **100 LumaCoins + two Hint charges**, once per local profile. Replay creates no second reward. If storage is full or a write fails, the interface reports the issue and retains the claim/retry path instead of reporting a gift it did not save. Practice consumes no campaign power stock and does not complete a Journey board.

The lesson-4 gate regression is corrected at the input and visual-instruction layers:

- A gate hit target carries its direction explicitly, so beginning a gate interaction reserves that target instead of treating it as an ordinary scroll candidate.
- The gate coach demonstrates a stationary tap. Piece coaches can still demonstrate a directional drag; the input required by a gate is visually distinct.
- Portrait practice provides a preview/open call to action at logical **y = 575**, outside the board. The compact two-column layout provides its corresponding preview/open control beside the board.
- The player previews, confirms opening, then exits the arrow. Immediate gate consequences remain visible before committing.

Ordinary taps and scroll starts use a common **22 logical-point** threshold; the former 10–22 gap is removed. Directional piece drags require their actual arrow direction. These are layout-coordinate thresholds after scaling, not universal physical millimetres. Reserved gates accept bounded drift without turning into a scroll. The native [1.8 failure record](validation/v1.9/ui-baseline-lesson4/README.md) explains why an accessibility click alone did not cover the old defect.

## Play, help and a fair action target

The board shows actual pieces remaining and the consumed/available action budget. Switches expose the immediate opened/closed gate preview. A supported closed gate can be reopened for one action. Free Undo reverses a productive action and refunds that action even at zero remaining moves; a failed blocked probe remains charged unless an existing protective effect absorbs it. Free restart is always a recovery option. The game never needs a purchase to make a level solvable.

`JourneyExperience` makes the rating explicit:

| Rating | Action rule after a solved board |
| --- | --- |
| 3 stars | No extension, and used actions ≤ `minimum + floor((initial budget − minimum) / 2)` |
| 2 stars | No extension, and used actions within the initial budget |
| 1 star | An extension was used |

Unlimited friend challenges retain three stars. Hint count, elapsed time and raw Undo count are not inputs. Undo can restore the action threshold by refunding the reversed productive action; it does not erase extension history. Stored best stars are preserved rather than retroactively recalculated. The threshold appears in the briefing so players can understand what three stars asks of them before playing. [Policy and fault tests](validation/v1.9/rewards/IMPLEMENTATION.md).

The native interface keeps asynchronous plan work separate from animation and touch. Guidance can identify a piece **or a typed manual-gate action**. A highlight is an explanation aid; it does not quietly perform a move. Power stock is reserved/completed/cancelled through the existing transaction path so a repeated tap does not apply one claimed effect twice.

## Preview, restoration and storage recovery

The actual L11 playthrough reproduced a preview whose confirmation controls could sit outside a scrolled compact viewport. The final preview is positioned against the visible viewport and retains both **Confirm and Cancel** within reach. The raw [offscreen-preview failure](validation/v1.9/ui-sdk-final-full100/README.md) remains; the current passing touch run exercises preview, cancel, confirmation and double confirmation. Locked level controls also use concise level labels so their text does not obscure navigation.

Guardian rotation now restores the visible encounter together with its saved state. Saving on exit must succeed before navigation proceeds. Guardian taps use their real typed hit targets, so a global Retry cannot be intercepted by underlying board coordinates. A failed local transaction enters a full-screen retry barrier around render, style synchronization and resume: repeated failed flushes remain visibly retryable rather than crashing or allowing another debit/claim/navigation. [FINAL5 storage evidence](validation/v1.9/ui-sdk-final5-storage-fault3/README.md) exercises two genuine permission-denied write cycles, exact pending-action recovery and once-only reward claims. It does not establish process-death recovery for a receipt that existed only in memory.

## A visible path from victory to reward

Rewards now starts with the **next Journey gift**: actual clears, milestone, named item, quantity and a real claim button. That is the destination advertised by Home, and victory can claim the same ready gift directly. The Shop shortcut remains available. A preview uses the milestone's frozen eligibility; returning later does not change the promised item. Existing receipts retain their historical contents.

The next section offers **one free charge of each unlocked power**, claimed explicitly once per local profile. The unchanged entering-level unlocks are Hint 1, Vision 5, Flow 12, Shield 25, Focus 45 and Trail 75. The trial remains available if a player postpones it or if stock is full. Unlocking, rendering, signing in or visiting a page does not automatically grant units. Trial receipt and stock credit are written together; failed storage does not consume the opportunity.

Victory shows actual coins/XP and stars, a ready gift or distance to it, an available sample or visual-style route, an unlocked guardian route when relevant, Next, Rewards and Map. The controls are invitations, not another mandatory reward modal before every board. The seven-visit daily gift cycle and daily/weekly quests keep their own rules; an absence does not erase an earned Journey gift or reset the visit cycle.

LumaCoins remain earned, local currency, distinct from account LumaGold. Trial charges and guardian rewards do not create premium currency or imply a paid transaction. Prices, receipt limits and the commercial qualification boundary remain visible.

## Optional guardian encounters

The separate guardian route contains **100 encounters**, opening one per **ten distinct Journey clears**, through 1,000. They do not replace an L/M/T board or block the main route. Home and victory can offer an available encounter; returning to Journey remains possible.

From guardian 1, some pieces have **two layers**. A displaced backing layer and `×2` badge distinguish their thickness: the first productive action peels a layer, and a later legal action moves the remaining piece. The work counter includes layers as well as movement, so a peel is real progress rather than a fake “almost won” animation.

From **guardian 5, after 50 Journey clears**, marked pieces move **one cell per action**, then leave the board at the edge. Footprint/step marks and accessible descriptions distinguish them from one-action exit pieces. Wrong-direction drags do not activate them. Hints identify the next productive piece; free Undo and restart remain available at zero budget.

Solving an encounter exposes an explicitly claimed **100 LumaCoin** reward once per encounter. Replaying cannot grant it again. Session state includes repeated piece IDs because a layered/cellwise piece can legitimately be acted on more than once. The [independent oracle](validation/v1.9/journey/guardians/summary.json) checks all reachable productive states of all 100 encounters; the [store tests](validation/v1.9/journey/guardian-store/summary.json) cover the once-only grant contract. These are local game contracts, not server-side purchase or anti-cheat claims.

## Companion expression and scenery

The existing **24 companions × 12 visual treatments** produce **288 combinatorial variants**. Styles are Plush classic, Cyberpunk neon, Retro arcade, Modern pixel, Sakura, Ocean, Space, Steampunk, Candy cloud, Halloween, Valentine and Ice crystal. Every treatment is free; it does not unlock a companion that the profile does not own, replace a wardrobe ownership receipt or alter an item's effect.

The render families change geometry and line treatment, not only palette: plush vector forms, comic outlines and explicit pixel grids. Each style has its own accessory silhouette. The five poses are Idle, Think, Happy, Gate and Win. These are original code-drawn combinations, not 288 independently hand-painted assets or art copied from another game. Existing clothes and Head/Aura/Charm equipment remain distinct collections with their stated ownership/effect rules.

Parallax follows **actual progress**, with restrained scenery away from central reading surfaces. It never advances the saved journey by itself or moves a hit target. Reduced motion freezes bob, rotation and parallax, including progression-driven scenery translation; fixed semantic landmark details may still reflect a completed milestone. Music, effects, haptics and reduced motion retain their own controls.

The renderer reuses its Paint and Path rather than allocating bitmaps per frame. [Host contracts](validation/v1.9/avatar/README.md) cover all combinations, colour-independent geometry and reduced-motion determinism. A native PNG art atlas is pixel evidence for the renderer, while screenshots of the integrated UI are separate evidence; neither establishes animation frame rate or human appeal.

## Remaining limits and acceptance

The first-100 [paired audit](validation/v1.9/journey/comparison.json) deliberately finds **identical puzzle geometry, budgets and independent action outcomes** before/after. L23 still rises from structural score 17 to 85; other abrupt changes also remain. Better briefing, preview, replayable lessons, visible help and optional encounters are the release response. No claim is made that human frustration or a Candy Crush-like difficulty curve has been calibrated away.

The [2026 primary-source dossier](research/2026-new-player/README.md) motivates testing different experience, schedule, social and accessibility contexts. Its aggregate surveys are not weights for a demographic difficulty model. Six synthetic behaviour profiles use identical parameters under adult Gen Z and older-adult labels. No real D1/D7, spending propensity, vulnerability classification, clinical benefit or future sales is inferred.

The [FINAL5 native qualification](validation/v1.9/SDK_UI_FINAL.md) separately records 2,277 full tutorial-to-100/mechanics checks, 85 true-first-install checks, 524 actual storage-failure checks and seven production startup/audio checks. It covers real gate drift and drags, compact practice, reward/trial claims, guardian layer/step/rotation/retry recovery, styles and six languages. Source tests and exported art atlases are separate evidence. Exact artifacts, signing and publication status belong to the release records; native success is not a claim of human retention or live commerce.
