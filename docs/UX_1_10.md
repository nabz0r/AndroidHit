## An arrival with a useful next step

Home separates the title, earned-coin wallet and settings. The account card distinguishes a guest from a provider-reported signed-in session; it uses no invented login state. The visible claim count opens the ready-to-collect list. The current Journey or saved attempt remains the main play action.

A chosen collection item appears with its real progress, ownership state and acquisition route. Without a chosen item, the card points to genuinely available rewards or the next activity. Opening an item or viewing its source does not buy or equip it. The Today and Prism journal cards connect the daily route and the longer Journey without creating an expiring objective.

The bottom bar keeps the parent destination selected on its subpages: Collection for wardrobe/styles, Events for objectives, and Journey for the journal/guardians. Final rc3 native navigation and PNGs cover these placements on the recorded emulator. Physical-device comfort, large fonts and TalkBack remain separate acceptance.

Selection needs both a correct accessibility state and a visible surface. The rc1 review reproduced seven direct colour calls receiving palette indices instead of ARGB colours; their controls were functionally selected but visually transparent. The rc2 source resolves those indices through the palette before drawing. Final rc3 independently checks 14 selected surfaces in actual screenshot pixels; every sampled colour is opaque mint `ffe1fff2`, alongside the accessibility selection state.

Footer reachability also needs actual bounds, not merely an accessible node name. In rc2, the wardrobe's inventory action extended below its content clip, the journal lost part of its lower controls and the inventory pager lacked a few logical points. The rc3 source increases the wardrobe, journal and Home content extents and raises the pager. Final rc3 scrolls to all four footers; each observed visible target is 48.36 dp high and entirely above the fixed navigation. This repairs visibility; it adds no reward, mechanic or progression state.

## One companion, seven visible slots

The shared [CompanionPortrait](../app/src/main/java/com/androidhit/lumadrift/ui/CompanionPortrait.java) composes the chosen companion and free visual treatment with four cosmetic slots—hat, dress, shoes, gloves—and three existing equipment slots—head, aura, charm. The latter retain their existing stated, bounded effects. Visual treatments grant no benefit and do not unlock an unowned companion.

The portrait supplies the same underlying appearance for Home, inventory, wardrobe and previews. A preview receives a caller-supplied visual snapshot. It grants nothing, writes nothing and cannot equip an unowned object. The caller distinguishes preview, equipped, owned and locked states and must enforce ownership before applying a look. Item art and acquisition are separate facts: seeing an outfit is not owning it.

The final source uses a light pastel native-dialog theme with plum text and a mint action colour, connecting preview controls to the Canvas palette. Home's journal tile uses a short next-level detail instead of squeezing the narrative sentence into a small tile; the visit-cycle note wraps across lines. Current native PNGs show these changes on the exact rc3 binary; the intermediate dark-dialog and truncated-tile images are not current release evidence.

The body, clothes and equipment move together; reduced motion freezes their transforms. Equipment glyphs differ in geometry, not only colour. The renderer reserves space around the full dressed character and reuses its drawing objects. The [host checks](validation/v1.10/avatar/README.md) cover 1,440 companion/style/pose command streams, 18 equipment glyphs, 24 cosmetic overrides, invalid inputs and reduced-motion determinism. These are not raster screenshots or frame-rate measurements. The renderer's [real Android API compilation](validation/v1.10/avatar/android-api-compile.json) is also separate from an app install.

## A collection goal chosen by the player

One item from the existing 24 wardrobe cosmetics or 18 equipment items can be pinned. Its catalog identity and progress remain local. Source routes distinguish Journey clears, weekly wins, event stars, visit rewards and earned-coin purchases. A completed goal remains chosen until explicitly changed; it does not automatically equip the reward or buy another item.

An owned item may have a recorded acquisition source, including an earned cosmetic capsule. This does not infer an account receipt for a locally earned item or fabricate a purchase. A preview can include an unowned item while its application remains gated by actual ownership.

## Objectives and real unclaimed rewards

The objectives page separates Today, This week, Journey and Ready to collect. It reads existing receipts, first-clear quest progress, event completion bonuses, Journey gifts and free power samples. Ready counts exclude already claimed rewards. A full power inventory does not turn a retained Journey gift into a lost gift. Claim actions recheck their captured day/week so an old screen cannot claim a new period's reward under an old label.

The seven-visit cycle retains its place across breaks. There is one visit gift per local date; it is not a seven-day attendance deadline. Daily and weekly event completion bonuses retain their existing 100/250 LumaCoin amounts. Their persistence repair is covered separately from layout: the [objective model tests](validation/v1.10/objectives/model-tests.json) exercise actual store/model APIs with preference doubles, concurrent claimers and retry boundaries. No 1.10 native filesystem-fault pass is claimed: those selectors remain blocked. The source persistence results do not become real disk-failure or process-death evidence.

The event page places its next-stage action before the stage grid and shows the existing completion bonus beside its state. The shop separates power packs, appearance items and the account LumaGold wallet. No live ad, checkout, paid draw or provider qualification is activated by this interface update.

## A journal grounded in existing progress

The [Prism journal](PRISM_STORY_1_10.md) tells ten original chapter introductions, objectives and endings across the ten existing Journey biomes. Journey progress and claimed optional guardian fragments are separate indicators. Chapter 1 is visible immediately; subsequent chapters open at each hundred distinct Journey clears. Guardians retain their original ten-clear unlock intervals and remain optional.

The journal reads actual Journey clear counts and claimed guardian receipts. It grants no currency, fragments, progress or equipment. A solved but unclaimed guardian is not a collected fragment. The read-only model passes [308,412 source assertions](validation/v1.10/prism-story/summary.json) across all 1,001 clear-count boundaries, receipt variants, ten chapters and six languages. The final native run separately visits all ten chapters and six languages. Neither coverage establishes human comprehension or narrative appeal.
