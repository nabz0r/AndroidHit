# Luma Drift 1.15 — relay Journey and dedicated destinations

The new main Journey uses the separately identified **U** family. Its 1,000 boards include thirty introductions followed by combinations of blockers, keys and relays. A large arrow gives a piece’s exit direction. A relay’s smaller arrows identify the gates it changes when that piece exits. A key leaving dissolves its matching seals. Gates are changed by those pieces; touching a closed U gate only explains the rule.

## Decisions and recovery

A successful exit removes one piece. Choose the order so later pieces keep a usable path and exit. A legal move can leave the position unsolvable; the game explains the cause and offers correction. Rejected taps are free. Every U exit can be undone and refunded, repeatedly, in the same attempt. Restart is free. No manual gate-opening action, paid continuation or hidden automatic exit is added to U.

The U action ledger counts committed exits for exact restoration. Completion always consists of one final exit per piece, with refunded corrections removed from that count; three stars are therefore a completion reward rather than a separate Undo/time efficiency rating. Hints and powers remain explicit inventory decisions. The original N/L/M/T and earlier content keep their own rules and identifiers.

## Destinations

- **Adventure:** the next Journey board, immediate Play/Resume, full map, events and rewards.
- **Play:** the board, its goal, Undo/restart and an illustrated power tray. Global navigation and wallet offers are absent.
- **Home:** living room, bedroom and garden, with a room camera and a separate decoration mode.
- **Collection:** characters, styles, equipment, powers and an enlarged wardrobe preview. Looking, acquiring and equipping remain distinct actions.
- **Atelier:** three visual furniture groups, item previews, earned-coin purchases and placement.

The phone presentation is portrait, including reverse portrait where supported. Short windows allow bounded browsing scroll; the main game prioritizes the board and essential controls. Full tablet/desktop and landscape compositions are not claimed.

## Larger home

Each of the three rooms is a **10 × 10** floor. Zoom controls and dragging while zoomed use the same transform as drawing and hit-testing. The catalogue has **42 different furniture designs**, including 30 new objects such as an aquarium, piano, greenhouse, library, bay window, telescope, fireplace, arcade cabinet, pond and canopy bed. Each has two drawn orientations. Existing four wall finishes and four floors remain available.

A design is owned permanently. There can be one instance of that design in each room. Buying grants ownership; placing happens only after a separate preview and confirmation. Rotating or cancelling a draft does not charge coins. Stored furniture remains owned. Changing rooms cancels the current placement draft.

The three rooms are available directly; the release does not claim earned room-extension progression. Existing 6 × 6 placements retain their exact coordinates in the larger living room, along with all previous purchases and balances. Other rooms start with explicitly free objects. Local ownership and layout writes retain their atomic failure/retry behavior.

## Compatibility

A saved Journey with content version6 retains the N board. New Journey starts use content version7 and U boards (engine rule version6, budget policy5). The current level’s original geometry is not substituted during restoration. Challenge identifiers continue to resolve their historical content. New shared boards use the U prefix.

To try U immediately with an older active save, tap the current level's numbered node in Adventure (or choose a board through the full map). Resume and Restart on the retained older attempt preserve that attempt's original rules.

Google identity does not synchronize local game or home saves. Release binaries retain the existing test-ad/checkout settings. Package, installed native and live-provider evidence are separate.
