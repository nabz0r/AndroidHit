# N Journey curve: independent design review

Read-only review of the frozen [authoring CSV](audit-learning-authoring.csv), not a player study. No catalogue changes. `D` counts prefixes of one optimal certificate with an alternative productive action that increases completion cost. Score, Hard/Expert and Recovery are authoring heuristics, not measured human difficulty. Recovery means a score below80% of the preceding board; it can still be Hard.

The defensible description is **progressively broader mechanical capacity, greater density and generally more costly choices, with local recoveries**. It is not monotonic by level or even by successive large windows: N401–600 is structurally easier than N241–400 despite its larger grid and nine switches. More pieces or a longer certificate alone does not imply more reasoning.

## First peaks, jumps and recoveries

- Cost-sensitive choices start at **N17**. N21 adds a second switch (score17→43, D1→2); N28 adds the three-switch/key combination. Both follow isolated component lessons.
- First Hard is **N54**, score81. N54–58 are five consecutive Hard boards, ending at103. N58→59 drops **103→20**, while minimum actions rises16→18: a longer board can be a structural recovery.
- The largest positive score steps are **N230→231:73→107 (+34)**, **N92→93:57→90 (+33)** and **N120→121:22→53 (+31)**. The first Expert is **N233**, score112, followed by N234 score56.
- The largest recovery by score is **N99→100:107→20**. Across the whole route the maximum adjacent piece increase is2 and minimum-action increase4. These bounds do not bound every aspect of attention: dependency depth jumps **7→13 at N239→240** and **5→11 at N999→1000**.

## Remaining design limits

**No new rule type appears before its isolated introduction** in this N corpus: exits, blockers, key/seal, manual gate and simple switch are separated; every switch changes one gate. Increasing multiplicity remains an observation target: two keys first appear at **N171**, without another isolated lesson specifically for two key identities.

**N31 remains a presentation test point.** N30 has two legal initial exits and needs no manual opening on its optimum; N31 has zero initial exits and requires three manual openings. The rule was taught at N13–16, but the combined all-gates-closed opening is not separately rehearsed. Its low score15 must not be read as proof that a novice understands where to begin.

**Late pacing is not yet a measured success.** There are89 labelled recoveries overall, but **N801–953 contains153 consecutive Hard/Expert labels**, and **N861–951 spans91 boards without a labelled Recovery**. N302–383 also contains82 consecutive Hard/Expert labels. Some relative recoveries can remain Hard, so these counts do not prove exhaustion; they do show that the total recovery count alone cannot establish a well-spaced rhythm. The final N1000 is Normal by score35 despite28 necessary actions and dependency depth11: it is not structurally the route's largest choice peak.

These are specific remaining design risks, not logical impossibilities or predicted player failure rates. The native1,000-board replay can qualify execution and recovery rules; first-time comprehension and whether this rhythm feels satisfying remain separate observations.
