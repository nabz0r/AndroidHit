## Findings and disposition

**House:** piano, aquarium, bookcase, sofa, arcade machine and record player are visibly enlarged and individually distinguishable. The original 1.15 furnished screenshot was also viewed for comparison. Outer furniture/room edges can leave the close-up viewport; the whole-view and zoom controls remain readable. The historical upgrade/pan/overview captures show the whole cutaway and the opposite side. That intentional camera framing is distinct from a cropped control. The selected-aquarium decorating toolbar has readable Cancel, Rotate, Store away and Place actions.

**Identity and collection:** French/Italian normal and French compact player portraits leave space between hat and nickname. The selected flag and three visually different badges are clear, and the nickname action is readable. Halo's ring silhouette, the different companion shapes, and full outfit looks are distinguishable in the inspected originals. Primary button faces, secondary controls and active navigation are visually distinct; this is not a measurement of every screen's contrast.

**Reveal correction:** Star parade/Parade stellaire had boots overlapping its title in candidate A. Candidate B leaves a visible gap in the inspected English/French upgrade/cold frames and French/German/Italian normal and compact frames. This finding is closed. The actual B draw shows a new Halo and a duplicate Star parade with compensation, rather than an invented outcome.

**Pools and localization:** Portuguese/Italian probabilities wrap within their columns, with separated name and rarity rows. Some long item names are ellipsized in catalogue cards; full titles are available in previews. All six additive companion banners show the correct selected tab and three companion silhouettes. Normal banners clearly display the 180 LumaCoins price and earned-coin text. The inspected signature banners show a different full outfit and a 260 LumaCoins price at normal size.

**Compact pages:** lower profile flags, reveal actions, atelier entries and collection cards extend below the initial compact viewport. Their initial screenshots show page-scroll cutoffs; they do not establish simultaneous visibility of every lower action. Native scrolling/touch assertions are documented separately. Home room tabs, decorating action, zoom/overview and navigation remain readable in the inspected compact frames. No blocking control overlap was found.

Minor refinements remain: decorative orbit lines/sparkles occasionally cross behind a label without preventing reading; the French/German hug action and Portuguese signature tab shorten with an ellipsis at compact size. Some historical preparation frames retain a temporary equipment/badge notice; those are unsuitable public screenshots, while settled browse/cold frames have no persistent notice.
