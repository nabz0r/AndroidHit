# Luma Drift 1.20 — readable kawaii loop

This iteration responds to the actual player report: missing victory feedback, hidden move/time display, automatic three-star grades, dense RPG-style navigation, text-heavy claims, tiny avatar reactions and no session pacing. It keeps the puzzle and owned character art, and changes the surrounding interaction.

## The loop

1. Home shows the real captain and team, one Play action, gifts that are actually earned, and a visible illustrated store. House and atelier remain secondary destinations.
2. Persistent navigation exposes LumaCoins, account-confirmed LumaGold (or an explicit unknown state), and session energy. They are different balances. A puzzle victory awards LumaCoins, not purchased LumaGold.
3. The puzzle shows its remaining action reserve and active elapsed time. The clock pauses with the app/help/pause and does not decide stars.
4. Real player exits make the team jump; three consecutive exits cheer and six dance. Blocked inputs and undo reset the reaction sequence. These are responses to actions, not fake near-misses or claims about a player's skill.
5. Victory remains until the player chooses a destination. The avatar celebrates, earned stars appear in sequence, and the displayed coin total counts toward the committed balance. A saved run-bound receipt prevents regranting rewards after restart.
6. Gifts open into a short animated reveal only after durable success. Already claimed gifts have no claim action. Home badges count real unclaimed rewards.

## Session energy

Ten units, one regenerated per ten minutes, maximum ten. A new attempt costs one from campaign U11 onward; tutorial/U1–U10 and friend practice stay free. Resume of the same attempt costs nothing. A restart is a new attempt and must display its one-unit price. A pending debit is recovered against the persisted board UUID after process death. Energy lives on this device, separate from Google account identity and server gold; it is not cross-device anti-cheat.

An optional ten-unit refill costs 50 LumaGold. Its quantity and price are explicit before confirmation. The pack may be used only when energy is empty, so a paid unit is not silently discarded at the cap. Production checkout and the new server energy SKU remain gated until provider qualification; a Lab test wallet is separate from earned coins. The server owns premium debit and inventory receipts. No real purchase is claimed by local simulation.

## New attempt action policy

Legacy saved attempts retain their policy. New relay attempts use policy6: physical refusals are free; undo itself is free but the earlier exit is not refunded. Exiting the same piece again spends another action. The minimum solution remains the tile count; the initial correction allowance is retained. Three stars require the minimum productive exits; two allow half the correction reserve; larger correction counts or continuations give one. This makes grading truthful without adding time pressure.

## Reference mechanisms, checked 29 October 2026

Epic's [quest guide](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/a23969068?lang=en-US) exposes quests from the lobby, with rewards tied to actual completion. Its [Locker documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/locker) separates appearance inventory from the lobby's other jobs. These support the decision to centralize navigation while keeping one task per destination; they are not evidence of retention for this game.

King's [Sugar Stars guide](https://candycrush.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002087578-How-do-I-collect-Sugar-Stars) illustrates explicit recognition after a completed level and revisiting a result. Luma uses its own action-based grade and original presentation. None of these references establishes that a particular energy rate, animation or store placement will improve D1/D7 retention. That requires observation of real players.
