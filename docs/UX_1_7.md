# Luma Drift 1.7 — clear choices, recovery and ownership

**Candidate source contract · 27 October 2026.** Exact package/screens and executed Android acceptance belong in [validation/v1.7](validation/v1.7/README.md). Earlier [1.6 UX evidence](UX_1_6.md) retains its old package and layout scope.

## Distinguish a hard choice from a dead route

A gate switch can legally leave while making the remaining puzzle impossible. The previous immediate-stuck test only looked for zero legal exits. The new controller asks for exact guidance after an exit, Undo, restart, restored attempt or continuation, rather than running the search on every rendered frame.

| State | Meaning | Player-facing consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Playable | A complete route exists and remaining actions cover its exits | A hint may highlight a safe next piece in the actual current state |
| Needs actions | A route exists but the remaining allowance is too small | Explain the shortage; a hint must not imply that highlighting alone fixes it |
| Dead end | No completion exists with these remaining pieces and gates | Offer useful Undo recovery and free restart; extra actions alone cannot repair it |
| Solved | Every piece has left | Normal completion handling; no hint charge |

The hint is a visible pulse on a solver-safe next piece. It does not follow an obsolete initial certificate after the player changes the route. A dead or already unaffordable run cannot spend hint stock. Saved power reservation/application/commit and one-charge confirmation remain the economy contract. Focus/Trail and the other powers are separately acquired effects, not a requirement to solve a board.

The J13 audit reproduces a legal doomed prefix while another exit remains. It also proves a full ten-exit route inside the initial 13 actions. The user’s exact save was not available. [Coordinates, gates and recovery](validation/v1.7/board-audit-journey13.md).

## Explore a real thousand-level route

The Journey map uses stable virtual coordinates for all positions 1–1,000 and draws its visible range. It scrolls instead of replacing the route with ten-level pages. A current-level return and direct level jump complement touch scrolling. Looking at a locked position never unlocks it or spends currency.

Ten 100-level biome ranges change the tint, world name and original procedural arrangement: First Light, Mint Meadows, Peach Coast, Cloud Gardens, Crystal Lagoon, Star Orchard, Moon Festival, Pumpkin Grove, Rose Aurora and Dream Summit. A visible world transition describes atmosphere; it does not create new puzzle identities, difficulty settings or paid gates. Remix remains its separate 240-board route.

Friend markers can only represent real returned profiles with mutual friendship and opt-in sharing. Do not populate an empty map with fictional friends or imply that a stale snapshot is live presence. Missing service/authentication must remain visible. Social progress sharing is not a cross-device save or authoritative completion check.

## An avatar with visible possessions

The new dressing room separates four **appearance-only** slots: Hat, Outfit, Shoes and Gloves. A native preview shows the equipped combination on the companion, alongside power stock information. Each card must state its name, slot, rarity, ownership and real acquisition condition. Owned and equipped are distinct states; selecting an unowned preview does not manufacture ownership or spend automatically.

The 24 wardrobe items include permanent Halloween and Valentine designs, starter clothes, daily-gift unlocks, weekly/event progression and fixed earned-coin shop choices. They add no puzzle, reward, stock, price or probability advantage. [Catalogue and exact thresholds](ECONOMY_1_7.md).

Existing Head/Aura/Charm equipment is a different inventory category with 18 previously shipped items and capped benefits. Keep its effect/source copy visible instead of relabelling those old items as cosmetic-only. Companion identity and themes are also appearance choices. A wardrobe count is not the count of all companions, equipment and powers combined.

## Return without a lost-day penalty

The daily gift panel presents seven visits with exact LumaCoins amounts. A claim advances by one visit, at most once per local date; a missed day does not reset the cycle. Show today's collected state and the next available reward without a false countdown promise or invented streak.

Daily and weekly quests have separate sections, progress/target, exact reward and claimed state. Daily objectives use 3 different first clears, 6 first-clear stars and 1 event clear; weekly objectives use 10 / 24 / 3. Old-board replays do not count as new wins on a later period. A successful claim cannot pay twice after recreation. Local date/clock limitations are stated in [the economy contract](ECONOMY_1_7.md).

## Two currencies and a reviewable cosmetic draw

LumaCoins are the local earned balance. LumaGold is the separate premium account wallet. The shop distinguishes their names, source and availability. A sign-in badge is not evidence of a loaded premium balance, and an unavailable real checkout must not use a fake Play price or successful purchase state.

The earned capsule confirmation states **150 LumaCoins**, one appearance-only item, common/rare/epic **60%/30%/10%**, per-item **7.5%/3.75%/1.25%**, and duplicate compensation **25/60/100 LumaCoins** by rarity. Those are actual unconditional catalogue probabilities. Cancellation spends nothing. A stable draw request cannot be retried for another result or another debit. The result explains new ownership or the exact duplicate compensation. There is no cash exchange or resale.

The prepared native account vault displays a real authenticated server catalogue, conditional account probabilities and account ownership. APK and server gates both remain disabled by default. The account and local ownership ledgers differ: a design already earned locally can be new to the account, so an account guarantee is not a promise of local novelty. An earned capsule must never silently spend LumaGold, cross into real billing or be marketed as an active paid gacha. No retention or conversion improvement follows merely from implementing this interface.

## Friends and bounded gifts

The social screen prepares account identification, friend-code requests, mutual acceptance, opt-in progress sharing, removal and bounded one-hint gifts. It must show authentication/service failures honestly and guard the captured account against late asynchronous responses. It does not request the address book or send external messages on the player's behalf.

A claimed server gift has an account/gift identity. Local receipt handling applies one hint stock unit exactly once, never currency. Full stock does not consume the local receipt, so the reception check remains retryable. Real two-account acceptance, server deployment and production identity are separate qualification steps.
