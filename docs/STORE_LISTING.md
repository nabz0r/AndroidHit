## Positioning and current English description draft

**One line:** Soft little worlds. Every move matters.

> Follow a little arrow into a softer world.
>
> Tap a piece or swipe along its arrow. Clear its path, release a key and choose when to change the gates. Switch previews show which edges will open and close before you confirm.
>
> Explore 1,000 Journey puzzles and a separate 240-board Remix route. Reopen a closed gate for one move. Undo is free and returns the action you take back, even when your remaining moves reach zero. Every fixed board has a solution within its starting allowance without purchases or powers; thoughtful choices still matter.
>
> Learn by playing seven small guided boards. Try a real blocker, key, gate, switch, Undo and Spark. Pause, resume the same step or replay whenever you like. Practice spends no inventory and changes no campaign progress. Finish all seven for a one-time local gift of 100 LumaCoins and two Sparks.
>
> Follow the map through ten atmospheres with original synthesized music. Dress your companion with a hat, outfit, shoes and gloves from 24 appearance-only cosmetics. Existing Headwear, Aura and Charm equipment remains a separate collection with its stated limited effects.
>
> Collect gifts over seven visits without losing the cycle when you miss a day. Discover daily and weekly puzzles and quests. Earn LumaCoins by playing, then choose direct items or a 150-coin cosmetic capsule with its item odds and duplicate compensation shown before confirmation.
>
> Unlock six powers as you progress. Read each effect, check your stock and confirm a charge. Spark can guide a piece exit or a gate opening; it does not play the action for you. Share a challenge code through Android without granting progression rewards to the recipient.
>
> Play in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese or Italian, with separate music, sound, haptic and reduced-motion settings.
>
> Progress, earned currency and items stay on this device. Optional Google identity does not synchronize the gameplay save. Online friend/gift acceptance remains under qualification in this candidate. It uses test ads and keeps real checkout and paid cosmetic draws closed. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local saves.

This is copy for the named candidate. Native-speaker review, final Play media exports, console declarations and review remain separate. A later commercial build must replace only the claims its actual qualified configuration changes.
